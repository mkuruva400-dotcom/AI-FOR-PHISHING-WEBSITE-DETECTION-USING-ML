Machine Learning & Deep Learning Engineering Insights
1. Ensemble Trees vs Single Models

Random Forest and XGBoost excel at detecting complex non-linear combinations of lexical features (e.g. high entropy combined with specific TLD and lack of HTTPS) without suffering from vanishing gradients.

2. Deep Learning ANN Generalization

The 4-layer Keras Artificial Neural Network incorporates BatchNormalization and Dropout to regularize feature weights, successfully preventing overfitting while modeling high-dimensional correlations across 24 input features.

3. Real-Time Inference Throughput

Tree-based models evaluate in under 3 milliseconds per URL, making them exceptionally well-suited for high-throughput perimeter firewalls and proxy inspection gateways.
